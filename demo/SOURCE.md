# Scripted demo audio source

The bundled `sample_public_domain.wav` is a 75-second excerpt from Kristen McQuillin's LibriVox reading of Chapter 1, “Down the Rabbit-Hole,” of Lewis Carroll's *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*. The work and recording are identified as public domain in the United States by the LibriVox catalog.

- LibriVox / Internet Archive item: https://archive.org/details/alice_in_wonderland_librivox
- Original recording: https://www.archive.org/download/alice_in_wonderland_librivox/wonderland_ch_01_64kb.mp3

The WAV is scripted demonstration audio, not a live streamer recording.
